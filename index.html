Winfu

Windows 上的个人影音库播放器 · A personal media library player for Windows

Winfu 把电脑上的文件夹、网盘挂载盘或 Google Drive 里的电影和剧集整理成海报墙，用 mpv 播放，并记住观看进度。所有片库数据、设置和观看记录都保存在用户自己的电脑上。

Winfu organizes movies and TV shows from local folders, mounted network drives or Google Drive into a poster library, plays them with mpv and remembers where you left off. All library data, settings and watch history stay on the user's own computer.

Google Drive

连接 Google Drive 时，Winfu 只申请只读权限（drive.readonly），用来列出用户选定文件夹里的视频、字幕和 nfo 文件，并在本机播放。Winfu 不会修改、删除或上传 Drive 里的任何文件。

When connected to Google Drive, Winfu requests read-only access (drive.readonly) to list the videos, subtitles and nfo files in folders the user chooses and to play them on the user's computer. Winfu never modifies, deletes or uploads files in Drive.
